How we're built

Four early decisions, made in your favour.

These aren't policies we adopted for marketing. They're structural — built into how each engagement is set up, so keeping them doesn't depend on goodwill.

01

Your keys, from day one

Work starts in your GitHub organisation and your cloud accounts — not ours. There is no handover at the end, because there is nothing to hand over: you've owned every commit since the first one.

Your repos Your cloud accounts Your credentials
02

The people you meet are the people who build

No sales engineers, no bench, no post-signature swap. The proposal names the engineers on your project — the same people on your first call, and later in your standups.

Named in the proposal In your standups
03

We'd rather lose the deal than pad the scope

Some of our first calls end with us recommending something smaller than what was asked for — occasionally something we don't sell at all. A right-sized project that ships beats a big one that stalls.

Built to run without us

How we work

A process you can set your watch to.

Every step is timeboxed and written down. You always know what happens next and what it costs.

  1. 01
    30 minutes

    Discovery call

    A free, no-pressure call. We assess feasibility, rough timeline and budget — and tell you if you don't need us.

  2. 02
    48 hours

    Written proposal

    Scope, milestones and a fixed price or transparent hourly rate. In writing, so you can compare it against anyone else.

  3. 03
    Weekly demos

    Build in the open

    Short sprints with a working demo every week. You have access to the repo and the board from day one.

  4. 04
    30-day support

    Launch & handover

    We ship it, document it and hand over the keys. 30 days of support included, then a retainer only if you want one.

Due diligence

Questions to ask any vendor. Including us.

Take these into every evaluation call you run. Under each one: the answer we'll give you.

You already have. The engineers on your discovery call are the ones named in the proposal and the ones in your repo. If the people change, the proposal changes — and you sign off on it.

Nothing. Code, infrastructure, credentials and documentation live in your accounts from day one. Parting ways costs you a goodbye call, not a migration project.

You watch it, rather than reading reports of it. A working demo every week, and standing access to the repository and the sprint board. If a week goes badly, you know that week — not at the deadline.

It gets quoted, in writing, before work continues. You approve the number, or we descope something else to fit. Nothing is billed that you haven't approved in advance.

Thirty days of support are included in every build. After that: a retainer if you want ongoing help, or a clean handover if you don't. Both are first-class endings — we design for either from the start.
