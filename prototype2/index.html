Act three — the proof

Shipped, measured, still running.

One engagement, told properly — then the shorter list. Outcomes are measured after launch, not projected before it.

Insurance• Claims platform• Case study

Claims intake, from a paper queue to same-day

The problem. A regional insurer's claims team was re-keying documents from email into a legacy system — a backlog measured in days, and error rates nobody wanted to measure.

What we built. A document-intake pipeline with human-in-the-loop review: extraction, validation against policy data, and a queue UI the team actually liked using. Deployed inside the client's cloud, handed over with docs and tests.

Why it stuck. The team could operate and extend it without us — which was the brief.
