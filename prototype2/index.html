02 Selected work

Shipped, measured, still running.

One engagement told properly, then the shorter list. Numbers are measured after launch, never projected before it.

Insurance · Claims platform

Claims intake, from a paper queue to same‑day

The problem. A regional insurer's claims team was re-keying documents out of email into a legacy system. The backlog was measured in days and the error rate was measured by complaints.

What we built. A document intake pipeline with human-in-the-loop review — extraction, validation against policy data, and a queue interface the team actually liked using. Deployed inside the client's own cloud account, handed over with tests and docs.

Why it stuck. Their team could operate and extend it without us. That was the brief, and it's the part we optimise for.

Deliberately uneventful.
