The commitments

The working agreement, in plain language.

Five clauses that shape every engagement. The contract version says the same things with more commas.

Working agreement — summary of terms

Applies to every engagement
Clause 01 Ownership

Everything is yours from the first commit

Work happens in your GitHub organisation and your cloud accounts. Code, infrastructure, credentials and documentation are yours throughout — there is no handover ceremony at the end because there is nothing of yours in our hands.

100%code & IP, yours
Clause 02 People

The engineers you meet are the engineers you get

The proposal names the individuals on your project. They are the people on your discovery call, and later in your standups. Any change of personnel goes through you, in writing, before it happens.

Namedin the proposal
Clause 03 Price

The number comes before the work

A fixed price or a transparent hourly rate, in writing within 48 hours of the first call. Scope changes are quoted and approved before work continues — nothing is billed that you haven't approved in advance.

48 hrsto a written price
Clause 04 Visibility

You watch progress — you don't request updates

A working demo every week, plus standing access to the repository and the sprint board. A bad week surfaces in that week's demo — never in a month-end surprise.

Weeklyworking demo
Clause 05 Exit

Leaving must cost you nothing

Because everything already lives in your accounts, walking away takes one conversation — there is nothing to migrate, export or unwind. Every build includes 30 days of post-launch support; a retainer after that is an option, never a dependency.

30 dayssupport included

No sales call required

Straight answers to the awkward questions.

The things buyers usually have to sit through a pitch to find out. All open, no accordion.

Q1Where are you actually based?

Gobichettipalayam, Tamil Nadu, India — and we keep deliberate overlap with US and UK business hours for standups, demos and anything urgent. Async by default, with a scheduled live window every working day.

Q2Who actually writes the code?

Senior engineers, named in your proposal. The people on your discovery call are the people in your repo — there's no swap to a junior bench after signature, because we don't have one.

Q3We already have a team. Does that work?

It's most of what we do. Our engineers work inside your repo and your rituals, not alongside them — and step out with 30 days' notice once you've hired.

Q4What does it cost?

A fixed price or a transparent hourly rate, in writing within 48 hours of the first call. If budget and scope do not line up, you hear it on that call — and if the honest number is smaller than you planned to spend, you hear that too.
